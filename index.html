DRIFT‑1

Three voices that never quite agree, three knobs, and no screen. Press play and shape the sound with the controls, or press into the surface.

How it works

Sound starts only when you press play. Use headphones for the low end.

Drag a knob up or down, or focus it and use the arrow keys.

Most synthesizers ask you to play notes. DRIFT‑1 plays one chord forever and lets you steer it. Set it on a desk, turn a knob a few degrees, and the room changes.

The controls

Three knobs. Every range is printed on the panel.

Drift

How far the three voices wander from pitch, and how fast the filter breathes. At zero it is a still chord. Turned up, it slowly falls apart and finds itself again.

Filter LFO
0.02 – 4 Hz
Voice detune
0 – 18 cents

Tone

Opens a resonant low-pass filter from a hum you feel more than hear, up to a bright edge. The chrome surface cools as the tone gets brighter.

Cutoff
280 Hz – 3.2 kHz
Resonance
Q 6, fixed

Space

Feeds a tape-style delay back into itself. Low settings add a short echo. High settings smear the chord into a wash that keeps ringing after you stop.

Delay time
420 ms
Feedback
0 – 82 %
